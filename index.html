Reference cards

🧘 The hourly unclench (10 seconds)
  1. Long, slow exhale.
  2. Scan the three zones: jaw → belly → butt.
  3. Let each one sag, like putting down a heavy bag.

Can't tell if your butt is clenched? Squeeze it deliberately for 2 sec, then release — the "after" feeling is the target. Trigger: your Garmin Move Alert buzz (or an hourly iPhone Shortcuts notification).

🌊 The urge wave
  1. Stop moving. Stand or sit still.
  2. Slow exhale; light lower-core lift (your "lift the sack" cue).
  3. Let the wave crest and fall — 30–60 seconds. It will fall.
  4. Still need to go? Walk calmly. No sprinting, no hard clenching.

No "just in case" wees — except before driving or filming (practical pass).

🚽 Toilet position + no straining
  • Squatty potty in place, knees above hips, lean forward, forearms on thighs.
  • Never strain. Slow exhale through pursed lips, or hum. Let the floor open.
  • Nothing in 5 min → leave, retry after next meal or a walk.
  • Never ignore the cue — when it calls, go. Best window: 20–30 min after breakfast + coffee.
🥄 Fibre dosing
  • Psyllium powder (home): start 1 tsp (~5g) once daily in a FULL glass of water (250ml+), drink immediately, chase with more water. After 1 week, if stools still sludgy → 1 tsp twice daily.
  • The water is not optional — psyllium without fluid makes constipation worse.
  • Capsules (out): follow packet equivalent of ~5g/dose, same water rule.
  • Kiwifruit: 1–2 green kiwis daily, breakfast or dessert.
  • Confirm dosing with your pharmacist/GP. Log every dose in Ingested → Medication & supplements.
🛏️ Down-training videos (10 min)
  • Hypertonic pelvic floor stretching & self-massage
  • 4 techniques to relax a hypertonic pelvic floor
  • Rotate them. Freestyle version: constructive rest breathing → knees-wide child's pose → happy baby. Inhale = ribs wide AND floor drops. Exhale = do nothing.
🎯 Benchmark battery — how to run it
  • Every 2–3 weeks. Same time of day, same clothes, tripods on the same marks (side + front angle).
  • Warm up 5 min (easy walk) first. It's a test, not a workout — stop anything painful.
  • Timed holds end when form breaks (hips drop, breath held, butt grips).
  • Left ankle: split-squat stance is your lunge; wobblier left balance is expected, not failure.
  • Film everything, enter the numbers here, send Claude the footage links/files for form review.
📏 The rules
  • Minimum day: 10-min video + one 10-min walk + log. Done = streak intact.
  • Never miss twice. One zero day is life. Two in a row triggers a minimum day, no negotiation.
  • Event/wedding days are zero days. Guilt is banned.
  • Day type = capacity, not location. Editing at home = Normal. Minimum is the escape hatch for genuinely depleted days.
  • Missed Pilates sessions roll forward. 3/week, whichever days they land.
  • Catch yourself butt-gripping or breath-holding in a class → stop, reset, go lighter.
