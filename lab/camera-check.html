Tests whether Chrome on this Mac can run two cameras at once. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

  2. Press Allow camera access and accept Chrome's prompt. The camera names then appear in both lists.

  4. Press Start both, wait 5 seconds, then press Copy report and paste it to Claude.
